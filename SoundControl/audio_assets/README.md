# Sound Control sounds

`mute.wav`, `unmute.wav` and `notice.wav` are original, deterministically
synthesized sounds generated by `generate_sounds.py`. No external recordings,
Discord sounds, samples or third-party sound libraries are used.

48 kHz, mono, 16-bit PCM. Two steady notes descend for mute and ascend for unmute;
notifications use two short glass-like taps. Each sound has a soft onset and tail.

These three sound recordings are dedicated to the public domain under CC0 1.0:
https://creativecommons.org/publicdomain/zero/1.0/
